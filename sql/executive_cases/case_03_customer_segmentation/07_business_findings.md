# Business Findings

## Executive Summary

Customer segmentation reveals that business value is highly concentrated within a relatively small portion of the customer base. High Value customers contribute a disproportionate share of total revenue and estimated lifetime value, while Medium Value customers represent the largest opportunity for sustainable revenue expansion. Low Value customers make up the largest share of the customer population but contribute comparatively less revenue.

## Key Findings

- High Value customers generate the largest revenue per customer and should remain the primary retention priority.
- Medium Value customers represent the strongest opportunity for upselling and long-term customer development.
- Low Value customers should be managed efficiently through scalable automated marketing programs.
- Customer engagement declines significantly as inactivity increases, creating measurable revenue recovery opportunities.
- Premium spending customers consistently demonstrate higher average order values and greater estimated lifetime value.
- Purchase frequency strongly correlates with customer lifetime value, making repeat engagement a critical business objective.

## Business Insight

Customer segmentation demonstrates that allocating marketing resources equally across the customer base is inefficient. Prioritizing investments according to customer value substantially improves expected marketing ROI while reducing unnecessary acquisition costs.
