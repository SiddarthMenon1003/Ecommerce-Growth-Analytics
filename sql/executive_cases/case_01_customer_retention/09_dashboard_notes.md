# Dashboard Design Specification

## Dashboard Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide executive leadership with a comprehensive view of customer acquisition, retention, purchasing behaviour, and customer lifetime value. The dashboard is designed to support strategic decision-making by transforming analytical findings into interactive business intelligence.

Rather than presenting operational reports, the dashboard focuses on answering executive-level business questions through clear KPIs, trend analysis, and customer segmentation.

---

# Target Audience

Primary Users

* Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
* Chief Commercial Officer (CCO)
* VP of Growth
* Head of Product
* Product Managers
* Business Analysts

Secondary Users

* Marketing Teams
* Customer Success Teams
* Strategy Teams

---

# Business Questions

The dashboard should enable leadership to answer the following questions:

* How many customers are being acquired over time?
* How effectively are customers being retained?
* What percentage of customers return for additional purchases?
* Which customer segments generate the highest lifetime value?
* Where should future retention investments be prioritized?
* How healthy is overall customer growth?

---

# Dashboard Structure

## Page 1 — Executive Overview

Purpose

Provide an immediate snapshot of overall marketplace health.

KPIs

* Total Customers
* Total Orders
* Total Revenue
* Average Order Value
* Customer Lifetime Value
* Repeat Purchase Rate

Visuals

* KPI Cards
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Monthly Customer Growth
* Revenue by State
* Customer Distribution Map

Business Question

How is the marketplace performing overall?

---

## Page 2 — Customer Retention

Purpose

Understand customer retention behaviour after acquisition.

KPIs

* Monthly Retention Rate
* Active Cohorts
* Customer Churn
* Returning Customers

Visuals

* Cohort Heatmap
* Retention Curve
* Monthly Retention Trend
* Customer Activity Matrix

Business Question

Are customers returning after their first purchase?

---

## Page 3 — Repeat Purchase Analysis

Purpose

Evaluate customer purchasing frequency.

KPIs

* One-Time Customers
* Repeat Customers
* Average Purchases
* High-Value Customers

Visuals

* Purchase Frequency Distribution
* One-Time vs Repeat Customers
* Top Repeat Customers
* Purchase Distribution Histogram

Business Question

How loyal are our customers?

---

## Page 4 — Customer Lifetime Value

Purpose

Identify customers generating long-term business value.

KPIs

* Average Customer Value
* Highest Customer Value
* Revenue per Customer
* Customer Lifespan

Visuals

* Customer Value Distribution
* Revenue by Customer Segment
* Top 20 Customers
* Lifetime Revenue Scatter Plot

Business Question

Who creates the greatest long-term value?

---

## Page 5 — Executive Insights

Purpose

Translate analytical findings into actionable business decisions.

Visual Components

* Executive Summary
* Key Findings
* Strategic Recommendations
* Priority Matrix

Business Question

What actions should leadership take next?

---

# Global Filters

The dashboard should support interactive filtering by:

* Purchase Year
* Purchase Month
* Customer State
* Product Category
* Payment Type
* Customer Segment

All dashboard pages should respond dynamically to selected filters.

---

# Design Principles

The dashboard should emphasize clarity and executive usability rather than visual complexity.

Guiding principles include:

* Minimal visual clutter
* Consistent color palette
* Clear KPI hierarchy
* Interactive drill-down capability
* Executive-first storytelling
* Consistent navigation across pages

Every visual should answer a specific business question and directly support strategic decision-making.

---

# Expected Business Outcomes

The completed dashboard should enable leadership to:

* Monitor customer acquisition performance
* Identify declining customer retention
* Evaluate customer lifetime value
* Prioritize retention initiatives
* Monitor repeat purchase behaviour
* Support long-term revenue growth strategies

The dashboard should function as a decision-support system rather than a reporting interface.
