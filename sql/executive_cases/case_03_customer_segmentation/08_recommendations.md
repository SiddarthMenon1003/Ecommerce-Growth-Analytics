# Executive Recommendations

## Recommendation 1

Increase retention investment for High Value customers through loyalty programs, personalized rewards, and premium customer experiences.

---

## Recommendation 2

Develop structured upsell and cross-sell campaigns targeting Medium Value customers to maximize long-term customer lifetime value.

---

## Recommendation 3

Launch automated lifecycle campaigns for Low Value customers to improve efficiency while minimizing marketing costs.

---

## Recommendation 4

Deploy win-back campaigns targeting Dormant customers with historically high lifetime value before focusing on new customer acquisition.

---

## Recommendation 5

Prioritize marketing budget allocation using estimated customer lifetime value instead of customer volume.

---

## Expected Business Impact

- Improved customer retention
- Higher customer lifetime value
- Increased repeat purchase frequency
- Better marketing budget utilization
- Higher long-term revenue growth
- Stronger personalization across customer segments
