# Case 01: Customer Retention Analysis

## Executive Summary

Customer retention is one of the strongest indicators of sustainable business growth. While acquiring new customers increases short-term revenue, long-term profitability depends on retaining existing customers and encouraging repeat purchases.

This analysis investigates customer purchasing behaviour to understand why retention is declining, identifies the key drivers behind repeat purchasing, and recommends data-driven initiatives to improve long-term customer value.

---

# Business Context

The company has experienced consistent customer acquisition growth over the past two years through expanding marketplace operations and marketing investments.

Despite this growth, executive performance reports indicate a decline in customer retention. Although new customers continue to enter the platform, fewer customers are returning to make additional purchases. This trend increases customer acquisition costs, reduces customer lifetime value (CLV), and creates greater dependence on continuous marketing spend to sustain revenue growth.

Leadership has commissioned a detailed retention analysis to identify the underlying causes of declining repeat purchases and to develop actionable recommendations for improving customer loyalty and long-term profitability.

---

# Step 1 — Data Validation

## Objective

Before analysing customer retention, the analytical warehouse was validated to ensure the dataset was complete, consistent, and suitable for business decision-making.

The validation process focused on confirming data integrity rather than generating business insights.

## Validation Performed

The following quality checks were completed:

- Verified total customer and order counts.
- Confirmed there were no duplicate customer records.
- Confirmed there were no duplicate order records.
- Verified that every order belonged to a valid customer.
- Checked for missing customer identifiers.
- Validated purchase timestamps.
- Reviewed order status distribution.
- Reviewed monthly order volume for continuity.

## Outcome

All validation checks passed successfully.

The warehouse is considered reliable for customer retention analysis.

---

# Business Question

**Why is customer retention declining, and what actions can be taken to improve repeat purchasing behaviour and long-term customer value?**

---

# Business Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Measure current customer retention performance.
- Quantify repeat purchasing behaviour across customer cohorts.
- Identify customer segments with the highest churn risk.
- Evaluate customer lifetime behaviour and purchasing frequency.
- Understand the relationship between retention and revenue growth.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to improve customer loyalty.

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following metrics will be used throughout this analysis:

- Customer Retention Rate
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Monthly Active Customers
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Average Purchase Frequency
- Average Time Between Purchases
- Revenue Retention
- Customer Churn Rate

---

# Analytical Approach

The investigation will follow a structured analytics workflow:

1. Validate data quality and warehouse integrity.
2. Perform exploratory analysis on customer purchasing behaviour.
3. Construct customer cohorts based on first purchase month.
4. Measure retention across monthly cohorts.
5. Analyse repeat purchase frequency and customer lifetime.
6. Identify high-risk customer segments.
7. Translate analytical findings into business recommendations.
8. Estimate the potential business impact of the proposed initiatives.
