# Case 04: Order Journey & Fulfillment Analytics

## Executive Summary

Traffic and customer acquisition continue to grow, yet revenue growth has slowed and customer satisfaction metrics are under pressure. Leadership suspects that operational inefficiencies in the order fulfillment pipeline are contributing to poor customer experiences, negative reviews, and lost repeat purchasing opportunities.

This analysis investigates the end-to-end order journey from purchase through delivery and review to understand where operational bottlenecks exist, how they affect customer experience, and which improvements should be prioritized.

---

# Business Context

The company processes nearly one hundred thousand orders across multiple Brazilian regions. Each order passes through a series of fulfillment stages: purchase, payment approval, carrier handoff, customer delivery, and review.

Delays at any stage can affect customer satisfaction, increase support costs, and reduce the likelihood of repeat purchases. Leadership requires a data-driven understanding of fulfillment performance to inform operational improvements.

---

# Business Question

**How efficiently does the business move orders from purchase to successful delivery, where are the operational bottlenecks, and how do those bottlenecks affect customer experience?**

---

# Business Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Measure fulfillment performance across the entire order journey.
- Identify operational bottlenecks at each stage of the fulfillment funnel.
- Quantify the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
- Evaluate SLA compliance and late delivery impact.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to improve operational efficiency and customer experience.

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Fulfillment Days (end-to-end)
- Approval Hours
- Shipping Processing Days
- Delivery Days (transit)
- SLA Compliance Rate
- Late Delivery Rate
- Review Score Distribution
- Delivery Efficiency Score
- Funnel Stage Progression

---

# Analytical Approach

1. Validate data quality and mart integrity.
2. Perform exploratory analysis on order journey metrics.
3. Analyse funnel stage progression and drop-off.
4. Measure fulfillment timing and identify bottlenecks.
5. Evaluate delivery performance and customer experience impact.
6. Quantify business impact and prioritise operational improvements.
7. Translate analytical findings into business recommendations.
8. Estimate the potential business impact of proposed initiatives.

---
